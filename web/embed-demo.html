Your website, with the SmartUp assistant

This page stands in for a page of smartupacademy.org. The chat bubble in the corner is the embedded widget.

How to embed

Add one script tag before </body> and point data-api-base at the server that runs the API:

<script src="https://YOUR-API-HOST/widget/widget.js"
        data-api-base="https://YOUR-API-HOST"
        data-title="SmartUp Assistant"
        data-lang="auto"
        data-primary="#1f4ed8" defer></script>

Set CORS_ORIGINS=https://www.smartupacademy.org in the API's .env so only your site may call it.

Open the bubble (bottom right) and ask, for example, What does the course say about pricing? or מה שיעורי הבית של הרצאה 5?
